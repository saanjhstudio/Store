# Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Saanjh Studio is a small handmade shop. We collect very little, and this page explains all of it in plain language.

## What we record when you browse

We count visits so we know which pieces people enjoy. For each page you open, or piece you view or tap to order, we record:

- **Country.** Worked out from your connection by our hosting provider. We do not store your city or your IP address.
- **Device type.** Only whether you are on a phone or a computer.
- **What you looked at.** The page, and which piece you viewed or tapped to order.
- **Where you came from.** The website that linked to us, if any, such as instagram.com.
- **The time of the visit.**
- **A random visit ID.** Your browser makes it up so we can count how many different people visited. It is forgotten when you close the tab, and it is not linked to your name or anything else about you.

## What we do not collect

- Your name, email address, phone number or home address. We never ask for them on this site.
- Your IP address or exact location in our visit records. Our hosting provider has to see your IP address to deliver the site to you, but we do not store it in those records.
- Advertising or tracking cookies. We do not use tools from advertising companies.

## How ordering works

There is no checkout on this site. When you tap an order button, WhatsApp or Instagram opens with a message about the piece, such as its name and ID. On Instagram we copy that message to your clipboard so you can paste it into the chat. Whatever you send after that is covered by the privacy policy of that app, and we only see what you choose to send us.

## Cookies and saved settings

- We do not set cookies for visitors.
- Your light or dark theme choice is saved on your own device so the site remembers it.
- The random visit ID is kept only in your browser tab, until you close it.

## Services we use

- Cloudflare
- Turso
- Google Fonts

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

## How long we keep it

We keep visit records so we can see how the shop does over time. They cannot be tied to a person.

## Reviews

Reviews on this site show the name and city the customer gave us. We add a review only with the customer's permission. If you would like yours changed or removed, message us.

## Failed sign-in attempts

If someone enters a wrong password on the shop's admin sign-in, their IP address is logged. It is removed automatically after a day.

## Your choices

Visit records cannot be tied to a person, so we cannot look up the records that are "yours". They do not identify you. If you would like us to remove a review, or you have a question about this page, message us.

## Changes

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and the date at the top.

## Contact

Message us on Instagram or WhatsApp using the links at the bottom of every page.
