✦ The Story

Where Saanjh began

Saanjh — the Hindi word for dusk, that quiet golden hour between day and night. When everything softens, and the world holds its breath. That feeling is what this studio is built on.

What started as a personal way to process emotions through art slowly became something people wanted to hold onto — literally. A friend asked for a keychain. A cousin wanted a painting. And slowly, Saanjh Studio was born.

Every single piece here is made entirely by hand. No two are exactly alike. When you give someone a piece from Saanjh Studio, you're giving them something that existed only for them.
